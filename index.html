02 / INFORMATION

Data the game processes

Meta account information

When online leaderboard features are available, the game requests the signed-in account’s app-specific Meta User ID and username through the Meta Platform SDK. The ID is used to retrieve the correct account’s personal-best entry. The game uses account names to label online rankings; profile photos are not displayed.

Scores & player names

Local rankings store a name entered by the player, a score, level and mode, a run identifier, and a timestamp. Online Adventure score submissions include the score, level, run identifier, and numerical components for orders, cooking, speed, variety, and streaks. Names entered on the local submission board are not included in that online score metadata.

VR interaction data

Hand and headset tracking information is processed on the device to support reaching, grabbing, gestures, and placement. The game’s leaderboard submission does not include hand poses, headset poses, microphone recordings, or camera images.

Development diagnostics

Development builds can write device logs containing technical errors, tracking status, gesture measurements, and gameplay events. Logs copied for troubleshooting may contain information returned by the Meta SDK. These should be reviewed before sharing and are separate from leaderboard submissions.

03 / PURPOSE

Why this information is used

  • Operate the game’s hand interactions and world placement.
  • Save and display local scores on the headset.
  • Associate submitted Adventure scores with the signed-in Meta account.
  • Show global rankings and the account’s personal best for each level.
  • Diagnose technical problems in development builds.

Cozy and Tutorial scores are excluded from online rankings. Tiny Kitchen’s leaderboard integration does not use account information for advertising.

04 / SHARING

What other people can see

Online leaderboard information is sent to Meta’s platform services. A player’s Meta account name and score may be visible to other players through the leaderboard. Local names and scores can be seen by people using the same headset.

The current leaderboard integration uses Meta’s services directly rather than a separate Overtime Frameworks leaderboard server. If additional analytics, support, hosting, or other providers receive player data in a future build, this policy must be updated to describe them.

Meta’s processing is described in its Privacy Policy. Privacy requests about Meta’s own account or platform records may also need to be directed to Meta.

05 / STORAGE

Where data stays

Local ranking files are saved in the app’s storage on the headset. The current implementation retains up to five ranking entries per Adventure level, with a backup of the previous save file. Clearing the app’s data removes those local files; it does not remove online leaderboard entries.

Online leaderboard entries are managed by Meta’s services. A higher existing personal-best score is preserved when a lower score is submitted. Online entries are not automatically deleted when the app is uninstalled.

06 / AGE GROUPS

Children & age-appropriate features

Tiny Kitchen is being developed for a mixed-age audience. Meta accounts and platform permissions can affect which online features are available to a player.

The current prototype does not yet request the Meta account’s age category. Age-based checks and the handling of restricted or unknown age categories must be implemented and verified before the mixed-age online experience is distributed. This policy does not represent those planned checks as an existing feature.

If you are a parent or guardian with questions about a child’s data, contact the responsible individual using the privacy contact below. Do not send identity documents or a child’s exact birth date in an initial request.

07 / YOUR CHOICES

Access, questions & deletion

You can play Cozy or Tutorial without submitting a score to an online leaderboard. To remove local rankings from a headset, use the device’s app-data controls. Device backup services, if enabled, may retain separate backups under their own controls.

To ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of data handled by Overtime Frameworks, email bennyfathurr@gmail.com with the subject “Tiny Kitchen — Privacy Request”. Include the type of request and, for a leaderboard entry, the Meta username and relevant level. Never send your password or account access token.

We may request the minimum additional information needed to verify that a request concerns your data. Requests about children may be made by a parent or guardian. Where data is managed through Meta, we will explain which action we can take and which action may require Meta’s account or privacy tools.
